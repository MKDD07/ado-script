Assam Tea Festival

Date Will Be Announced Soon

Assam, Abhayapuri

28°C / Clear

Get ready to immerse yourself in the enchanting world of Assam’s finest teas at the Assam Tea Festival—a vibrant celebration of flavors, culture, and tradition! Set against the lush green landscapes of Assam’s legendary tea gardens, this festival is a paradise for tea lovers, adventure seekers, and culture enthusiasts alike.

From soul-soothing tea-tasting sessions to exhilarating plantation tours, the festival offers an unforgettable experience that captures the essence of Assam’s tea heritage. Witness the meticulous art of tea plucking, engage in interactive workshops with expert tea sommeliers, and savor the finest brews straight from the source.

But that’s not all! The Assam Tea Festival is more than just tea—it’s a cultural extravaganza. Enjoy traditional Assamese dance performances, folk music, craft exhibitions, and a feast of local delicacies that perfectly complement your chai experience. For the thrill-seekers, river cruises, golfing, and wildlife safaris add an adventurous touch to this tea-filled retreat.

Whether you're a tea connoisseur or simply looking for a refreshing escape, Assam Tea Festival promises a mesmerizing blend of taste, tradition, and tranquility. Mark your calendars and get ready to sip, savor, and celebrate in the land of the world’s best tea! 

Date & Venue of Assam Tea Festival

Assam Tea Festival is an amazing two-day event organised by the Government of Assam. Official dates are not announced yet, but the festival typically takes place in November and December.  

History of Assam Tea Festival 

The documented account of tea production in Assam can be traced back to the 18th century when India was invaded by Britishers who later made India their colony. In the 18th century, Britishers started the large-scale production of Tea in Assam. Initially, the tea was produced by the Singpho tribe of Assam but later Britishers took over the Ahom kingdom through a treaty named Yandaboo Treaty. Besides taking over the kingdom, Britishers also involved themselves in the tea business and started trading the tea to other parts of the world.  

From then onwards, the tea industry flourished in the state and it garnered many national and international accolades for its premium quality, and therapeutic plus medicinal properties induced tea. Tea, over the years, has become an innate part of Indian hospitality, especially in north India. People of north India serve tea to their guests and just like India’s philosophy of ‘Unity in Diversity’ tea is also made with diverse proportions and recipes in every part of the country. The Assam Tea Festival is one of the popular festivals of Assam that primarily celebrates this journey of a tea seed that becomes a hot brewing cup of a warm welcome in Inda. The festival is celebrated in the winter season anytime between November to January every year. 

An interesting fact about Jorhat where the festival is celebrated is that this place is also home to Tocklai Experimental Station that was established in 1911, to conduct research and development activities on tea plants, seeds, and leaves.

Major Attractions of Assam Tea Festival in Jorhat

1. Gardens

Assam Tea Festival is an opportunity for the visitors to explore the tea estates in Assam and tea gardens or even understand the process of tea plantation by the local farmers. Tourists often buy tea leaves and seeds from the local farmers for their kitchen garden or as souvenirs.  

2. Fun Activities

The tea festival gives the tourists a chance to indulge in various activities like jungle safari, playing golf, eating delicious local Assamese food, shopping, and enjoying the cultural performances at the event.

3. Popular Assam Tea

The widely renowned Assam Tea is an indigenously grown black tea that is produced from a plant known as Camelia Sinesis. The tea is known to be full of antioxidants and for improving blood circulation in the body. Apart from the black tea one will also get to witness and taste white tea, green tea, oolong tea, and many other variants of tea at the festival.  

How to Reach Assam Tea Festival in Jorhat

Jorhat, the host city of the Assam Tea Festival, is well-connected by road, rail, and air. It has its own airport with flights from major cities like Bangalore, Kolkata, and Guwahati. Below are the best travel options to reach Jorhat conveniently.

By Air

Rowriah Airport (Jorhat Airport) is just 7 km from the city.

  • Connectivity: Delhi to Jorhat flights, and major cities as well.
  • Connecting flights: IndiGo via Kolkata/Guwahati from Delhi, Mumbai & Bengaluru
  • Taxis and buses are available for local transport. 

By Rail

Jorhat City Railway Station is the nearest railhead. No direct trains run from Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, or Bengaluru, so connecting trains are required.

  • From Guwahati: Jan Shatabdi Express, JTTN Intercity
  • From Dibrugarh: GHY Intercity
  • Taxis and buses are available from the station to the festival venue.

By Road

Jorhat is easily accessible via national highways. You can travel by inter-state buses or personal vehicles to enjoy scenic landscapes and roadside dhabas.

  • From Nagaon: 185 km via NH 715
  • From Shillong: 360 km via AH1/2 & NH 715
  • From Tezpur: 160 km via NH 715
  • From Dibrugarh: 140 km via NH 2
  • From Guwahati: 300 km via NH 27 & NH 715

Where to Stay?

Travellers wanting to visit Jorhat to attend the festival can book hotels in Jorhat for their own prefferences. Jorhat offers multiple hotels in every budget, ranging from budget accomodations to luxury experiences.  

Conclusion 

Assam Tea Festival is a wonderful celebration that brings people together to enjoy the delicious teas from the region. Held in the beautiful tea gardens of Jorhat, it's not just about the tea; it's also a time to appreciate the hard work of the people behind it. The festival is filled with fun activities and cultural performances that make it a joyful and vibrant event. Tea lovers from all over India come to taste and enjoy the various tea flavours on display. It's a special time to show gratitude, celebrate Assam's tea heritage, and enjoy the delightful atmosphere. Frequently Asked Questions About Assam Tea Festival 

Q1. When and where will Assam Tea Festival take place?
A1. Assam Tea Festival will be held in Jorhat, Assam, known as the tea capital of India. The exact dates will be announced soon, but it typically takes place in November or December.

Q2. What are the main attractions of the Assam Tea Festival?
A2. The festival offers tea-tasting sessions, plantation tours, cultural performances, craft exhibitions, adventure activities, and traditional Assamese cuisine. Visitors can also interact with tea experts and learn about the art of tea-making.

Q3. How can I reach the Assam Tea Festival?
A3. Jorhat is well-connected by road, rail, and air. The nearest airport is Rowriah Airport (Jorhat Airport) with flights from Kolkata and Guwahati. Jorhat City Railway Station is the closest railhead, and national highways connect it to nearby cities.

Q4. Is there an entry fee for the Assam Tea Festival?
A4. Entry to the festival is usually free, but some workshops and tea-tasting events may require prior registration or a nominal fee.

Q5. What accommodation options are available in Jorhat for festival visitors?
A5. Jorhat offers a range of accommodation options, including luxury hotels, budget lodges, homestays, and tea garden resorts, ensuring a comfortable stay for all visitors.
