Voyage of Royal Excellence

Maharajas' Express

The Maharajas' Express is globally celebrated as one of the world's leading luxury trains, redefining royal hospitality on wheels across Rajasthan and Northern India. Passengers journey through timeless destinations including Delhi, Agra, Ranthambore, and Jaipur, discovering iconic palaces, historic hill forts, and thrilling wildlife sanctuaries.

Aboard the train, guests experience the golden age of royalty with exquisitely furnished presidential suites, two thematic peacock and royal-court dining salons, and seamless 24/7 dedicated butler service catering to every comfort.

The Golden Chariot

The Golden Chariot opens an enchanting window into the rich cultural legacy and natural majesty of Southern India. Traveling across vibrant landscapes from Bengaluru and Mysore to the UNESCO ruins of Hampi and the sunny shores of Goa, the voyage reveals ancient stone temples and lush coastal beauty.

Designed with inspirations drawn from traditional Dravidian and Hoysala architecture, the train features deluxe guest carriages, rejuvenating Ayurvedic spa facilities, and curated regional dining that immerse travelers in South Indian grandeur.

Buddhist Circuit Tourist Train

The Buddhist Circuit Tourist Train takes travelers on an inspiring spiritual pilgrimage following the sacred enlightenment trail of Gautama Buddha. The itinerary connects revered pilgrimage destinations including Bodh Gaya, Rajgir, Nalanda, Varanasi, Sarnath, and Buddha’s birthplace in Lumbini.

Equipped with modern air-conditioned coaches, tranquil dining spaces, and thoughtful amenities, the train provides a deeply peaceful journey, allowing pilgrims and cultural travelers to meditate and discover historic stupas and monastic treasures in comfort.
